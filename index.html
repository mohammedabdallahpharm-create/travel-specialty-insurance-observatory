DECISION RIGHTS

Four roles. Not necessarily one organisation.

A provider may administer a claim without holding authority to make the final binding decision. Delegation must be evidenced, not assumed.

01Risk carrierUnderwriting & policy liability
02AdministratorIntake, processing & coordination
03Decision-makerBinding approval / decline authority
04Accountable partyGovernance & customer outcome
!

Travel and Pet claims workflows are researched separately. A TPA appointment, assistance contract or claims submission portal does not by itself prove delegated final decision authority.

TECHNOLOGY / WORKFORCE

AI in the workflow.
Humans in control.

Separate document automation, decision support and delegated automated decisions. The difference is operational — and material.

01Process automation

Capture, OCR, extraction, classification and routing.

02Decision support

Summarise evidence, flag exceptions and suggest next actions.

03Delegated automated decision

Approve, decline or settle within defined authority.

04Autonomous high-impact decision

Material claim decision without meaningful human involvement.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

AI prepares the work. Humans make the decision.

The research question is not simply whether AI is used. It is what authority the system has, who can intervene, and who remains accountable when an outcome is wrong.

Evidence shown here includes UAE insurance examples outside Travel and Pet. It is contextual evidence, not proof of deployment in either domain.

HOW WE WORK / RESEARCH METHOD

Evidence before
interpretation.

A transparent process for turning market observations into defensible intelligence — without overstating what the evidence can say.

01Define the question

Start with a specific operational or market question.

02Collect evidence

Prioritise primary sources and record dates, context and limitations.

03Test interpretation

Separate verified facts, unknowns and competing hypotheses.

04Decide the status

Publish, continue research, monitor, reject or archive.

05Maintain the trail

Link the registry record to its detailed research and source trail.

Evidence statuses

VERIFIEDDirectly supported by reliable evidence for the stated claim.
PARTIALLY VERIFIEDSome elements are supported; material questions remain unresolved.
UNVERIFIEDIdentified, but not sufficiently corroborated.
HYPOTHESISAn explanation to be tested, not a fact.
DISPUTEDCredible sources conflict or the claim is contested.
HISTORICALAccurate for a past period; current status not confirmed.
NON-NEGOTIABLE

A technology announcement does not prove production deployment. A job description does not prove actual workflow. A TPA appointment does not prove delegated decision authority.
