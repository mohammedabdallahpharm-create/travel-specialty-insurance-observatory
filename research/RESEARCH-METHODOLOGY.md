# Research Methodology — v1.0

## Purpose
Produce traceable, evidence-led intelligence about UAE travel and companion-animal insurance operations, organisational responsibilities, technology and workforce change.

## Research cycle
1. Define a specific research question.
2. Identify the observed event or market signal.
3. Collect primary sources first; add credible secondary sources for context.
4. Record source title, publisher, URL, publication date, relevant passage or data, and access/observation date.
5. Separate direct evidence from interpretation.
6. Record verified facts, unestablished claims, competing hypotheses and evidence gaps.
7. Assess confidence and limitations.
8. Decide whether the finding qualifies as a signal, requires more research, or should be rejected.
9. Update the structured registry and detailed Markdown record together.
10. Revisit follow-up indicators and review dates.

## Evidence status
- Verified: directly supported by reliable evidence sufficient for the stated claim.
- Partially Verified: some elements are supported; material elements remain unresolved.
- Unverified: a claim has been identified but not sufficiently corroborated.
- Hypothesis: an explanatory proposition to be tested, not a fact.
- Disputed: credible sources conflict or the claim is materially contested.
- Historical: accurate for a past period but not confirmed as current.

Statuses are not interchangeable. A signal may contain facts with different evidence statuses; state the status at claim level where necessary.

## Source hierarchy
Prefer legislation, regulators, official registers, policy wordings, insurer or provider disclosures, tender documents, audited reports and direct statements. Use credible journalism and industry analysis as supporting context. Social posts and job advertisements are leads, not proof of internal operations.

## Operational discipline
- A technology announcement does not prove deployment, production use or measurable outcomes.
- A job description does not prove actual workflow or decision rights.
- Do not infer contractual responsibility without evidence.
- Distinguish insurer, underwriter, broker, TPA, claims administrator, assistance provider and decision-maker roles.
- Keep event date, publication date and observation date separate.
- Record source limitations and contrary evidence.
- Never fabricate metrics, sources, market coverage or operational claims.

## Signal decision
Possible decisions: Publish, Continue Research, Monitor, Reject, or Archive. The decision must be explained in the detailed record.
