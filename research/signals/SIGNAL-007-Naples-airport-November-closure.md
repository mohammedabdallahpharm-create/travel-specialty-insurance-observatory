# SIGNAL-007 — INT-001: Naples Airport November closure

**Observed:** 23 November 2026 | **Evidence:** Partially Verified | **Decision:** Continue Research

GESAC announced that Naples Airport would be closed to air traffic throughout November 2026 for runway and taxiway refurbishment. Some flights are transferred to Salerno Costa d’Amalfi Airport, which is scheduled to serve 15 domestic and international destinations during the closure. EUROCONTROL's event register independently lists the closure from 1 to 30 November.

## Verified
- Airport operator GESAC confirms full closure throughout November.
- Works include runway/taxiway refurbishment and airfield ground-lighting modernisation.
- Some flights are transferred to Salerno Costa d’Amalfi Airport.
- EUROCONTROL lists the closure period as 1–30 November.

## Insurance relevance
Potential airport substitution, ground-transfer expense and missed connections. These are hypotheses until linked to an individual itinerary and expenses. Airline rebooking/refunds and ground-transfer support must be checked first.

## Not established
No UAE-linked passenger, policyholder, claim or insured loss.
