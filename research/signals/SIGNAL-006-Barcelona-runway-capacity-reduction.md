# SIGNAL-006 — EU-002: Barcelona runway works and capacity reduction

**Domain:** Travel Insurance  
**Event period:** 16–26 November 2026  
**Announcement:** 18 September 2026  
**Observed:** 23 November 2026  
**Evidence status:** Partially Verified  
**Confidence:** High  
**Decision:** Monitor

## Signal
Aena announced planned runway works at Barcelona–El Prat. During the main impact period, 16–26 November, the airport is to operate with one runway. Aena forecast 1,441 fewer slots, approximately 15% of total capacity, and stated that airlines and slot coordinators had been informed and schedules adjusted in advance.

## Verified facts
- Aena published the works plan on 18 September.
- Main operational impact is 16–26 November.
- The airport is operating with one runway during the main impact period.
- Aena forecast 1,441 fewer slots (around 15%) and said schedules were adjusted in advance.

## Insurance relevance — hypotheses, not coverage conclusions
Planned capacity reduction may affect punctuality, cancellations or connections, but advance schedule changes may absorb much of the impact. Only actual itinerary-level disruption and unrecovered passenger expense can support an insurance-exposure assessment.

## Not established
No flight-level disruption count attributable to the works, UAE-linked passenger impact, policyholder, claim or insured loss has been established.

## Follow-up
1. Obtain Aena's post-26 November operational update.
2. Compare actual flight movements with published schedules.
3. Verify any airline notice attributing a specific disruption to the works.
4. Test any passenger expense against exact policy delay and missed-connection wording.

## UAE Travel Insurance Market Implications

**Exposure pathway:** Indirect  
**Event-to-market link:** Potential indirect itinerary link  
**Claims / insured-loss evidence:** Unproven

### Market exposure
Barcelona's planned runway-capacity reduction may affect UAE residents travelling to/from Barcelona or connecting through the airport. Advance schedule adjustment was announced, so operational impact cannot be assumed.

### Affected policyholders
Potential UAE policyholders with itineraries involving Barcelona during 16–26 November, especially passengers whose schedules changed or connections were disrupted.

### Insurance impact
Review delay, cancellation and missed-connection benefits only after confirming actual flight-level disruption. Planned works known before booking or policy inception may interact with known-event and foreseeability clauses.

### Claims impact
Potential delay, missed connection or extra accommodation/transport, but no attributable UAE-linked flight disruption or claim is recorded.

### Insurer impact
Potential low-level monitoring/customer-information requirement; no market-wide impact or claims surge is evidenced.

### Evidence required next
Post-26 November Aena update; flight-level actual versus scheduled movements; UAE-linked itinerary evidence; passenger expenses and policy purchase dates.

**Assessment:** Indirect UAE policyholder exposure through destination/connection itineraries; planned capacity reduction is not itself an insured loss.
