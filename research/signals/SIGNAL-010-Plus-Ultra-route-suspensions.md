# SIGNAL-010 — INT-004: Plus Ultra route suspensions and cessation risk

**Observed:** 23 November 2026 | **Evidence:** Partially Verified | **Confidence:** Medium | **Decision:** Continue Research

Plus Ultra has suspended multiple routes, including Lima–Madrid and Buenos Aires–Madrid from 17 November. Other Latin American routes were already suspended. As of 23 November, formal cessation had not been conclusively confirmed by the airline. Reports of staff being told not to report and aircraft returning to base remain press-reported; the airline website reportedly stopped selling tickets.

## Verified / carefully qualified
- Multiple route suspensions are reported.
- Ticket sales reportedly unavailable on the airline website as of 23 November.
- Formal cessation was not confirmed by the airline in the source reviewed.

## Insurance relevance
Potential refunds, alternative transport, stranded passengers and supplier-failure benefits. Airline insolvency cover must be checked separately from ordinary cancellation/delay cover; payment-card chargeback and travel-agent remedies may also matter.

## Not established
No formal legal cessation, passenger count, policyholder, claim or insured loss.
