# SIGNAL-010 — INT-004: Plus Ultra route suspensions and cessation risk

**Observed:** 23 November 2026 | **Evidence:** Partially Verified | **Confidence:** Medium | **Decision:** Continue Research

Plus Ultra has suspended multiple routes, including Lima–Madrid and Buenos Aires–Madrid from 17 November. Other Latin American routes were already suspended. As of 23 November, formal cessation had not been conclusively confirmed by the airline. Reports of staff being told not to report and aircraft returning to base remain press-reported; the airline website reportedly stopped selling tickets.

## Verified / carefully qualified
- Multiple route suspensions are reported.
- Ticket sales reportedly unavailable on the airline website as of 23 November.
- Formal cessation was not confirmed by the airline in the source reviewed.

## Insurance relevance
Potential refunds, alternative transport, stranded passengers and supplier-failure benefits. Airline insolvency cover must be checked separately from ordinary cancellation/delay cover; payment-card chargeback and travel-agent remedies may also matter.

## Not established
No formal legal cessation, passenger count, policyholder, claim or insured loss.

## Source
- Airspace Times, [Plus Ultra disruption tracker](https://airspacetimes.com/en/disruptions/plus-ultra-seeks-creditor-protection-and-drops-most-routes-2026-11-13/), updated 23 Nov 2026.

**Next question:** Has Plus Ultra formally ceased or entered insolvency, and what passenger refund/protection routes apply?

## UAE Travel Insurance Market Implications

**Exposure pathway:** Indirect  
**Event-to-market link:** Potential  
**Claims / insured-loss evidence:** Unproven

### Market exposure
Reported Plus Ultra route suspensions may affect UAE residents only if they booked the airline directly or through an affected codeshare/agency itinerary. No UAE route or policyholder link is currently established.

### Affected policyholders
Potential UAE residents with tickets on suspended Plus Ultra services or connecting itineraries involving the airline; exposure depends on ticketing carrier, operating carrier and point of sale.

### Insurance impact
Separate ordinary cancellation/delay cover from airline insolvency or supplier-failure protection. Confirm whether the policy expressly includes financial failure, when the failure must occur, and any purchase-date or booking-channel conditions.

### Claims impact
Potential unrecovered ticket cost, replacement transport, accommodation or stranded-passenger expense only if a covered event and eligible booking are established. Formal cessation/insolvency is not confirmed.

### Insurer impact
Potential supplier-failure enquiries and assistance coordination; no UAE policy exposure, claims or loss is evidenced.

### Evidence required next
Formal airline, regulator or court notice confirming legal/financial status; affected flight and ticket inventory; booking/payment channel; policy wording and alternative refund/chargeback remedies.

**Assessment:** Indirect supplier-failure watchpoint; route suspension is not proof of insolvency or an insured airline-failure event.
