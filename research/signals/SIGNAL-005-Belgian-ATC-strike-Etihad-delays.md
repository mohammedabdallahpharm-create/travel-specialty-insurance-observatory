# SIGNAL-005 — EU-001: Belgian ATC strike and Etihad delays

**Domain:** Travel Insurance  
**Event date:** 23 November 2026  
**Evidence status:** Partially Verified  
**Confidence:** High  
**Decision:** Continue Research

## Signal
Brussels Airport reported that the national demonstration involving some skeyes air-traffic-control staff would result in all arriving and departing flights being cancelled from 07:00 to 21:00 local time. Etihad independently confirmed that EY057 Abu Dhabi–Brussels was delayed due to Brussels ATC industrial action and that return flight EY058 would also be delayed.

## Verified facts
- Brussels Airport's operational notice confirms the cancellation window and warns of a particularly busy airport on 24 November.
- Etihad's flight-status notice confirms the disruption reason for EY057 and the knock-on delay to EY058.

## Insurance relevance — hypotheses, not coverage conclusions
Potential passenger consequences include missed connections, additional accommodation, meals and ground transport. Actual delay duration, itinerary structure, airline assistance and policy waiting-period thresholds determine whether a claim is even assessable. ATC industrial action must not be conflated with an airline's own-staff strike.

## Not established
Final flight times, missed connections, passenger expenses, UAE policyholders, claims and insured losses have not been established.

## Follow-up
1. Capture final EY057/EY058 movement times and the post-strike recovery.
2. Verify passenger-level missed connections and expenses.
3. Identify airline rebooking/refunds and assistance.
4. Compare exact UAE policy delay and missed-connection triggers.

## UAE Travel Insurance Market Implications

**Exposure pathway:** Direct  
**Event-to-market link:** Potential direct route/regulatory link  
**Claims / insured-loss evidence:** Unproven

### Market exposure
Etihad confirms disruption to EY057 Abu Dhabi–Brussels and the return EY058 due to Brussels ATC industrial action, creating a documented UAE-origin carrier connection.

### Affected policyholders
Potential UAE-based travellers on EY057/EY058 and connecting journeys affected by the final operating times.

### Insurance impact
Compare actual delay duration with policy thresholds (which vary by product), industrial-action definitions, pre-existing/announced-event exclusions and missed-connection benefits. Airline passenger-care and rebooking duties are separate from insurance.

### Claims impact
Potential delay benefit, missed connection, extra accommodation, meals or transport if the policy trigger is met and costs remain unrecovered. Final delay duration and claimant-level loss are not established.

### Insurer impact
Potential short-term assistance contacts and evidence review; no claims volume or insurer loss is known.

### Evidence required next
Final actual departure/arrival times; airline letters stating cause; onward itinerary and connection buffer; receipts and support/refunds; applicable policy schedule.

**Assessment:** Direct UAE-carrier route link verified; insured impact depends on final delay and passenger-level evidence.
