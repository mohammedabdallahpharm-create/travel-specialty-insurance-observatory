# SIGNAL-002 — UAE-001: Dubai airport disruption following extreme rainfall, April 2024

- **Domain:** Travel Insurance
- **Event date:** 16 April 2024
- **Publication date:** 23 April 2024
- **Market:** United Arab Emirates
- **Evidence status:** Partially Verified
- **Confidence:** High for airport operational facts; insurance outcomes not established
- **Research decision:** Continue Research

## Executive assessment

Dubai Airports' 23 April 2024 recovery statement documented the operational impact of the exceptional rainfall event. It reported 2,155 cancelled flights, 115 diverted flights and more than 75,000 welfare packs distributed to disrupted guests.

These are airport-operational and passenger-support facts. They do not establish the number of insured passengers, insurance claims or claim payments.

## Verified facts

- 2,155 flights were cancelled, as reported by Dubai Airports.
- 115 flights were diverted.
- More than 75,000 welfare packs were distributed to disrupted guests.
- Dubai Airports reported a return to normal operations by 23 April 2024.

## What is not established

- How many affected passengers held UAE-issued travel-insurance policies.
- The number or value of insurance claims arising from the event.
- Which individual expenses were met by airlines, airport support or insurers.

## Insurance and operations questions

1. Which passenger-care obligations applied to the affected itinerary?
2. What rebooking, accommodation, meals or transport were provided by the carrier or other parties?
3. Which expenses remained unrecovered and potentially relevant to policy benefits?
4. How do cancellation, delay and diversion differ under the applicable policy wording?

## Operational hypothesis — not a coverage decision

A multi-day airport disruption can create a complex chain of passenger support, airline recovery and potential insurance assessment. The claim decision still depends on the individual itinerary, policy wording, evidence and recoverable expenses.

## Evidence gaps and next action

- Review applicable UAE passenger-welfare rules and airline obligations.
- Identify primary policy wordings and compare delay/cancellation triggers.
- Locate public aggregate travel-insurance claims or assistance data, if available.
