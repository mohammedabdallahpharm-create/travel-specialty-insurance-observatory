# SIGNAL-009 — INT-003: Pakistan airspace restriction on Indian aircraft

**Observed:** 23 November 2026 | **Evidence:** Partially Verified | **Decision:** Monitor

Pakistan extended its restriction on Indian-registered aircraft and aircraft owned, operated or leased by Indian airlines using Pakistani airspace until 04:59 on 24 December 2026, according to reports quoting a Pakistan Airports Authority NOTAM. This is a continuation of an existing restriction, not a new closure on 23 November.

## Verified from reporting
- Reported NOTAM expiry: 24 Dec 2026 at 04:59.
- Reported scope includes Indian-registered and Indian-airline-owned/operated/leased aircraft.
- Alternative routings may increase flight time and operating costs, but passenger-level delay has not been established.

## Insurance relevance
Possible schedule extension, missed connections and delay benefits only if actual passenger itinerary times meet exact policy terms. Airline fuel cost is not an insured passenger loss.

## Evidence gap
The NOTAM itself was not directly retrieved in this research pass; this record relies on two reports quoting it.
