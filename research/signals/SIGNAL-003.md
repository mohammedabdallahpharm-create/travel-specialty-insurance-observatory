# SIGNAL-003 — UAE-002: GCAA temporarily suspends ADS-B OUT mandate amid GPS interference, June 2025

- **Domain:** Travel Insurance
- **Event / decision date:** 26 June 2025
- **Market:** United Arab Emirates
- **Evidence status:** Partially Verified
- **Confidence:** High for the regulatory decision; passenger-facing impact not established
- **Research decision:** Continue Research

## Executive assessment

The UAE General Civil Aviation Authority issued Safety Decision 2025-02 on 26 June 2025, temporarily suspending the mandatory ADS-B OUT requirement for specified operations within UAE airspace. The decision cited increased GPS interference/jamming affecting ADS-B OUT reliability and directed operators to use alternative approved surveillance means and comply with ATC instructions.

This is an aviation-system resilience and regulatory signal. It does not itself establish passenger cancellations, travel-insurance exposure or claims.

## Verified facts

- The decision applies to specified IFR operations within UAE controlled airspace and the Emirates FIR, and to air navigation service providers.
- The GCAA cited increased GPS interference/jamming as adversely affecting ADS-B OUT reliability.
- The decision temporarily suspended specified ADS-B OUT requirements and required alternative approved surveillance means.

## What is not established

- That the decision caused a specific passenger flight cancellation or delay.
- That a travel-insurance claim arose from the decision.
- That the measure represented a complete closure of UAE airspace.
