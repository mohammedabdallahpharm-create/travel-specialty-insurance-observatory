# SIGNAL-008 — INT-002: Chiang Mai Yi Peng aviation restrictions

**Observed:** 23 November 2026 | **Effective:** 24–25 November | **Evidence:** Partially Verified | **Decision:** Monitor

CAAT's AIP Supplement A 27/26 warns of sky-lantern releases in Chiang Mai and Lamphun and specifies periods affecting Chiang Mai Airport. It notes that lantern height and drift are unpredictable and may affect aircraft operations. Secondary reporting cites AOT figures as of 10 November: 62 cancelled, 90 retimed and 57 additional flights at Chiang Mai.

## Verified
- Official CAAT supplement applies 24–25 November 2026.
- Affected periods: 24 Nov 0300–0500 and 1200–1800 UTC; 25 Nov 1200–1800 UTC.
- CAAT identifies unpredictable lantern drift as an aviation operational risk.
- AOT figures are secondary-reported and must remain attributed to AOT.

## Insurance relevance
Potential schedule changes, missed connections and additional expenses. Advance schedule adjustment may absorb much of the impact. Confirm actual flight and passenger outcomes.

## Not established
No final flight-movement outcomes, passenger claims or insured losses.
