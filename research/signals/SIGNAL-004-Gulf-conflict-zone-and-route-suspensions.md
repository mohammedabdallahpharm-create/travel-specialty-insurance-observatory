# SIGNAL-004 — GCC-001: Gulf conflict-zone advisory and route suspensions

**Domain:** Travel Insurance  
**Event date:** 16 November 2026 (EASA revision); observed 23 November 2026  
**Evidence status:** Partially Verified  
**Confidence:** High  
**Decision:** Continue Research

## Signal
EASA extended CZIB-2026-07R4 through 31 January 2027. The bulletin identifies elevated risks over specified maritime airspace in the Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman and recommends avoidance for applicable operators, with caution and risk assessment over land. Etihad separately confirms Abu Dhabi–Riyadh and Abu Dhabi–Dammam passenger and cargo cancellations through 30 November.

## Verified facts
- EASA revision dated 16 November; validity through 31 January 2027.
- Scope includes Bahrain, Kuwait, Qatar, UAE and Oman FIRs, with specific maritime-airspace recommendations.
- Etihad confirms the two named Abu Dhabi–Saudi routes cancelled through 30 November; other Abu Dhabi–Saudi flights stated to operate as scheduled.
- GCAA's Iranian-airline suspension was announced 24 September and is background context, not a new event today.

## Insurance relevance — hypotheses, not coverage conclusions
Potential exposure includes rerouting, delay, missed connections, trip interruption and additional accommodation/transport. Airline refunds, rebooking and assistance may reduce residual expense. Policy purchase date, known-event provisions, war/hostilities exclusions, travel-advisory clauses and exact benefit wording require case-specific review.

## Not established
No affected UAE policyholder count, claims, paid losses, or policy-specific coverage outcome has been identified. The EASA notice is not a blanket closure of all Gulf airspace.

## Follow-up
1. Verify UAE-linked flight movements and actual diversions/cancellations.
2. Track EASA/GCAA and airline notices.
3. Obtain complete current policy wording and compare relevant exclusions and benefits.
4. Identify passenger expenses remaining after airline remedies.
