# Domain Blueprint — UAE-first

## Travel Insurance (TRAVEL)
Research coverage:
- Emergency Medical Coverage
- Trip Cancellation
- Trip Interruption
- Flight Delay
- Emergency Assistance
- Baggage Protection (subdomain: BAGGAGE)

Baggage remains within Travel until evidence establishes a distinct standalone insurance structure or market category.

## Pet Insurance (PET)
Initial scope: companion animals only.
- Accidental Injury
- Illness
- Veterinary Expenses
- Surgery
- Diagnostics and Medication
- Emergency Veterinary Care
- Third-Party Liability, where covered

Livestock and agricultural animal insurance are excluded from the MVP.

## Shared analytical lens
Both domains use a common research method while retaining domain-specific coverage, claims evidence, entities and operational questions.

## Initial priority: Claims Operations
Investigate intake, eligibility, documentation, evidence validation, exclusions, fraud controls, decision authority, settlement, appeals and closure. Do not assume the insurer, administrator, assistance provider and final decision-maker are the same entity.
