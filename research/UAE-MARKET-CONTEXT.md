# UAE Market Context

## Scope
The initial market is the United Arab Emirates. The Observatory will examine travel insurance and companion-animal insurance, with Claims Operations as the first research priority.

## Research status
This file is a context framework, not a completed market assessment. No market-size estimate, penetration rate, insurer count or market ranking is asserted here.

## Questions to establish with evidence
- Which entities are licensed to underwrite or distribute relevant products in the UAE?
- Which travel and pet insurance products are currently offered, and under what policy terms?
- Which entities administer claims, provide emergency assistance or deliver veterinary services?
- How do distribution channels and partnerships allocate operational responsibilities?
- What UAE-specific regulatory requirements apply to insurers, intermediaries and relevant service providers?
- What is publicly documented about claims processes, exclusions, service standards and customer recourse?

## Research controls
Use current primary sources wherever possible. Record jurisdiction, source date and applicability. Do not treat a product page, marketing statement or job advertisement as proof of end-to-end operating arrangements.
