# UAE Entity Registry — Initial Research (2026-11-11)

## Scope and limitations

This is an initial evidence-backed entity map for the Travel and Pet domains, focused on claims operations. It is not a comprehensive list of UAE market participants. Inclusion means that a role is supported by the cited public source; it does not establish every contractual or operational responsibility.

**Important distinction:** a claims submission channel, claims administrator, emergency assistance provider, underwriter and final claims decision-maker are different roles. Do not collapse them into one entity without contractual or operational evidence.

## Claims operations observations

### Travel — Allianz-branded product in the UAE

The published operating model separates at least two visible functions:
- **Underwriting:** Alliance Insurance Company P.S.C.
- **Policy servicing, claims administration and emergency assistance interface:** NEXtCARE.

The customer-facing process includes emergency triage and assistance, online non-emergency claim intake, document retention, claim tracking, and claim-specific reporting deadlines. This is evidence of a multi-entity service model. It is not evidence that NEXtCARE holds final coverage authority for every claim.

### Travel — ADNIC

ADNIC publishes a direct claims journey: incident reporting → document submission → claim assessment → claim reference and tracking. For baggage/flight claims, it requests policy details, flight details, estimated value of lost items and any airline compensation. For medical/accident claims, it requests incident details and supporting police/medical information.

The page tells customers to contact the assistance company named in their policy schedule/certificate for travel and medical emergencies, but does not identify that company in the cited claims guidance.

### Pet — MetLife

MetLife describes a reimbursement model: the customer visits a registered UAE veterinary clinic, pays the bill, submits an itemised invoice and receipt through MedNet, and receives reimbursement after approval. The page identifies MedNet as its claims partner. It does not say that MedNet makes the coverage decision. The published page states that approved claims are paid within seven days, subject to the required documents.

### Pet — MooPet

MooPet's claims page instructs customers to complete a claim form, have a section completed by the veterinarian, and email the form with supporting documents. It says covered claims are typically settled within ten business days after all information is received. The same page records an underwriting transition: Alliance Insurance for policies issued before 1 June 2026; QIC for policies issued on or after that date.

The email address uses the domain kainosint.com. This alone is insufficient to assign Kainos an administrator, adjudicator or delegated authority role. That identity and role remain unresolved.

## Cross-domain research implications

1. **Claims intake is not claims authority.** A portal or email route proves where documents are submitted, not who evaluates coverage.
2. **Assistance and reimbursement are different workflows.** Travel medical emergencies may require immediate coordination/direct billing, while baggage and other non-emergency losses use evidence-led reimbursement.
3. **Third-party operating models need contract-level research.** The public pages reveal service interfaces, but not service-level agreements, delegated claims authority, audit rights or escalation matrices.
4. **Evidence quality is a claims control.** Airline reports, police reports, travel records, itemised veterinary invoices and receipts are visible evidence requirements across the reviewed examples.
5. **Do not infer automation.** The existence of online portals and digital forms does not establish AI use, automated adjudication or straight-through processing.

## Priority evidence gaps

- Obtain and compare current policy wordings for each product, including the identity of the insurer and the claims clauses.
- Confirm the legal entity behind the MetLife pet product and whether any claims authority is delegated to MedNet.
- Identify the organisation behind the MooPet claims email domain and determine its contractual role, if publicly documentable.
- Identify the assistance provider named in ADNIC travel policy schedules and compare product-specific arrangements.
- Map the actual decision and escalation rights: who can request more evidence, approve, decline, reserve, settle and handle appeals?
- Review baggage claims in detail: airline Property Irregularity Reports, carrier compensation, proof of ownership/value, unattended-property exclusions and reporting deadlines.
