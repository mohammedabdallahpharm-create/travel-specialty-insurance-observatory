Human decision gate
The tool can flag a case for review. It cannot determine eligibility, interpret exclusions conclusively, approve or reject a claim, or promise reimbursement.
Data needed for a real deployment
Authorised policyholder itinerary data, reliable flight-status feed, versioned policy wording, airline assistance records, expense evidence and governed human review.

04 / What this prototype can and cannot show

Do not confuse a workflow demonstration with evidence of market adoption.

CAN TEST

Whether a public disruption signal can be translated into a structured case record.
Which passenger and itinerary facts are missing before exposure can be established.
Whether airline support and recoveries are captured before assessing residual expense.
Where policy wording and human review must enter the workflow.

CANNOT PROVE

That any UAE insurer currently operates this integrated capability.
How many insured travellers were affected in the historical cases.
Claim frequency, acceptance rates, paid losses or savings.
Whether a particular expense is covered under POLICY-A, POLICY-B or any actual policy.
Research status: Prototype v0.1 · Historical event facts and synthetic scenario inputs kept separate. Principle: AI prepares the work; humans make the decision.
